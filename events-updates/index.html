30 March, 2026| Baseline Survey at Eppalagudam under Kaveri University’s Village Adoption Initiative
As part of its Village Adoption Programme, Kaveri University successfully conducted a comprehensive baseline survey in Eppalagudam, the adopted village. The initiative actively engaged II-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture students, offering them valuable field exposure and hands-on experiential learning.
 
The survey was designed with two integrated components:
  • Participatory Rural Appraisal (PRA): A community-driven approach to understand village dynamics through active stakeholder participation
  • Socio-economic Survey: A structured, quantitative assessment conducted using a pre-tested questionnaire
 
Under the PRA framework, students undertook key field exercises including transect walks, resource mapping, Venn diagram analysis, seasonal calendars, and timeline analysis. These were complemented by a detailed household-level socio-economic survey to capture data on livelihoods, resources, and socio-economic conditions.
 
This blended approach significantly strengthened students’ understanding of rural systems by integrating qualitative insights with quantitative evidence. It also enhanced their capacity for problem identification, data-driven planning, and context-specific policy interventions.
 
The baseline survey provides a critical foundation for identifying priority areas in Eppalagudam. Based on the findings, need-based, location-specific developmental interventions will be designed and implemented in collaboration with the community.
 
Kaveri University sincerely thanks the residents of Eppalagudam for their enthusiastic participation, cooperation, and warm hospitality. Special appreciation is extended to the Village Sarpanch, Sri Nagi Reddy, for his continuous support and leadership in facilitating the successful conduct of the survey.

1 February 2026 Student- Led AI Seed Testing Project Earns Global Top-10 ISTA Ranking

Kaveri University Develops AI-Powered System for Automated Seed Germination and Variety Testing

Researchers at Kaveri University have developed an advanced artificial intelligence (AI)–based seed germination evaluation system that significantly enhances the accuracy, speed, and objectivity of seed quality testing in major crops such as paddy, bajra, and maize.

The innovative project, titled “Automated Seed Germination and Variety Evaluation in Paddy, Bajra, and Maize Using a Computer Vision–Based Germination System,” integrates AI-driven computer vision and deep learning models to overcome the limitations of conventional manual germination testing, which is often labour-intensive, time-consuming, and subject to human variability.

Designed in alignment with International Seed Testing Association (ISTA) standards, the system enables high-throughput, standardised germination analysis supported by complete digital documentation. A dedicated AI-powered application, developed using Python and advanced machine learning frameworks, automatically identifies key germination indicators such as radicle protrusion and seed coat cracking, ensuring consistent, objective, and reliable evaluation.

The research is being carried out by Mr. B. Koushik, a second-year B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture student, under the guidance of Dr. Darmagaru Shivani, Assistant Professor and Head, and Dr. A. Kavitha Reddy, Assistant Professor, Department of Genetics and Plant Breeding. The initiative is further strengthened through industry mentorship from Dr. Nookaraju Akula, AGM (Quality Control), Kaveri Seed Company Limited, reflecting a strong and impactful industry–academia collaboration.

The AI-based germination system has been tested on 62 seed varieties, including 32 paddy, 15 bajra, and 15 maize varieties, and has demonstrated an impressive accuracy of 99%. It generates automated germination reports and structured digital datasets, improving traceability and supporting data-driven decision-making in seed quality assessment and certification processes.

The project has also received global recognition, having been shortlisted among the top 12 projects worldwide from more than 280 international submissions at ISTA, and securing an overall global rank of eighth. Following this achievement, the research team has been invited to participate in the ISTA Annual Meeting 2026, scheduled to be held in Calgary, Canada, from June 21 to 26, 2026.

Future work will focus on expanding the AI model to additional crops and seed varieties, conducting rigorous statistical comparisons with manual testing methods, refining the application based on industry feedback, and strengthening ISTA-compliant digital documentation. A patent and licensing application for the developed AI-driven model has already been submitted and is currently under examination.

This breakthrough underscores Kaveri University’s commitment to advancing smart seed technologies and strengthening innovation in agriculture through AI-enabled precision testing systems.

January 2025 Turmeric value chain summit 2025

Turmeric Value Chain Summit 2025 | Hyderabad

The Turmeric Value Chain Summit 2025, held at The Park, Hyderabad, was a grand success, bringing together policymakers, industry leaders, researchers, startups, and farmers to deliberate on a future-ready roadmap for turmeric aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047.

Dr. V. Praveen Rao, Vice-Chancellor, Kaveri University, attended the summit as Advisor, CII Telangana Agri & Food Processing Panel and presented the Context Paper, setting the tone for the deliberations. His presentation highlighted India’s strengths in the turmeric sector, emerging opportunities, and the critical role of innovation across the entire value chain—from production to processing, value addition, and exports.

The summit was graced by Shri Tummala Nageswara Rao, Hon’ble Minister for Agriculture & Cooperation, Government of Telangana, along with the Principal Secretary (Agriculture), Chairman and Secretary of the National Turmeric Board, and eminent leaders from industry and academia.

With over 150 delegates participating, the summit witnessed rich and forward-looking discussions on production systems, processing technologies, value addition, quality assurance, exports, and startup ecosystems, reinforcing strong public–private–academic collaboration to enhance farmer incomes and strengthen India’s global leadership in turmeric.
