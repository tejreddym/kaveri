Programme Overview

Comprehensive 4-Year Agricultural Degree

The B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture program at Kaveri University is a multi-disciplinary undergraduate degree spanning 8 semesters. It integrates fundamental agricultural science with cutting-edge technology, sustainable farming systems, and practical field work.

Students undergo intensive hands-on training across crop production, soil science, plant protection, seed technology, agricultural economics, and farm machinery, preparing them for leadership roles in agribusiness, research, government services, and entrepreneurship.

Hands-on Experience

Practical & Experiential Learning

Learning at Kaveri University extends beyond traditional classrooms into active farm fields and research labs.

  • Student READY / RAWE: Rural Agricultural Work Experience embedded in final year semesters.
  • Experiential Learning Modules (ELP): Commercial seed production, bio-fertilizer production, and nursery management.
  • Industrial Internships: Field attachments with agribusiness corporates and research institutes.
  • Study Tours: Exposure to national agricultural research centers and progressive farming clusters.

Programme Structure

8 Semesters across 4 Years featuring credit-based coursework, practical labs, field work, and internship modules.

  • Semesters 1–6: Core Agricultural Courses, Practical Labs & Field Experiments
  • Semester 7: Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) & Agro-Industrial Attachment
  • Semester 8: Experiential Learning Programme (ELP) / Hands-on Business Modules

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet the official academic qualifications:

  • Pass in 10+2 / Intermediate examination from a recognized board.
  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology / Mathematics / Agriculture.
  • Minimum Aggregate: 60% marks in qualifying examination.
