Programme Overview

Transforming Farming with Digital Technology

The B.Sc. (Hons.) AgriTech program bridges the gap between traditional agricultural practices and modern digital innovation. Across 8 semesters, students learn how to apply sensor networks, automated irrigation, remote sensing, satellite imaging, and AI data analytics to optimize crop yields and soil health.

With strong emphasis on climate-resilient farming, protected cultivation, precision horticulture, and biotechnology, graduates emerge ready to lead next-generation AgriTech startups, smart farming ventures, and ag-industrial enterprises.

Precision Agriculture
Field robotics, drone spraying, and automated fertigation systems.
Industry & IBM Modules
Exposure to IoT data platforms, cloud analytics, and ag-fintech systems.

Programme Structure

4-Year / 8-Semester curriculum featuring engineering fundamentals, crop science labs, RAWE, and technology internships.

  • Semesters 1–5: Agricultural Sciences, Smart Tech & IoT Engineering
  • Semester 6: AgriTech Project & Industry Internship
  • Semesters 7–8: RAWE & Startup Business Incubator

Eligibility Criteria

  • Pass in 10+2 / Intermediate examination from a recognized board.
  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics or Biology / Agriculture.
  • Minimum Aggregate: 60% marks in qualifying examination.
