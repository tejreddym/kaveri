Excellence in Fruit, Vegetable & Ornamental Sciences

A specialized 4-Year undergraduate degree focusing on commercial production of fruits, vegetables, flowers, protected cultivation, nursery management, landscaping, and post-harvest technology.

  • Infrastructure: Greenhouse & Polyhouses

  • Duration: 4 Years / 8 Semesters
Programme Overview

High-Value Commercial Crop Sciences

The B.Sc. (Hons.) Horticulture degree at Kaveri University provides rigorous theoretical and practical education in the science and technology of cultivating fruits, vegetables, medicinal plants, aromatic crops, and ornamental flowers.

Students gain extensive hands-on experience in greenhouse management, drip irrigation, organic farming, urban landscaping, plant propagation, and post-harvest preservation techniques.

Protected Cultivation
Controlled-environment agriculture in polyhouses and shade nets.
Value Addition & Processing
Post-harvest technology, juice processing, and flower preservation.
Elective Specializations

Core Elective Areas

1. Fruit Science (Pomology)

Production, breeding, and orchard management of tropical, sub-tropical, and temperate fruit crops.

2. Vegetable Science (Olericulture)

Cultivation of high-yielding commercial vegetable crops, seed production, and hybrid breeding.

3. Floriculture & Landscaping

Commercial cut flower production, greenhouse floriculture, nursery management, and landscape design.

Programme Structure

4-Year / 8-Semester ICAR-aligned curriculum with field attachments, polyhouse labs, and nursery training.

  • Semesters 1–6: Fruit, Vegetable, Flower & Post-Harvest Science
  • Semester 7: Rural Horticultural Work Experience (RHWE)
  • Semester 8: Commercial Horticulture Experiential Modules

Eligibility Criteria

  • Pass in 10+2 / Intermediate examination from a recognized board.
  • Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology or Mathematics / Agriculture.
  • Minimum Aggregate: 60% marks in qualifying examination.
